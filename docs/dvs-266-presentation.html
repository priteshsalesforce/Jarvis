Customer Value & Job(s) To Be Done

What is the value to the customer?

Employees
Time back — less portal-hopping. One brief, not 13 interruptions.
The business
Fewer routine HR/IT tickets reach the service desk.
Managers
Time on exceptions and readiness, not repetitive approvals.
Salesforce
Owns the employee action layer — expands beyond HR/IT Service.

What are the Job(s) to be Done?

When my day gets disrupted, reshuffle it quietly so I don't lose 20 minutes apologizing.
When messages pile up, draft the replies in my voice — I'll approve in one batch.
When an action affects other people, wait for my explicit OK, and let me undo it.
Every morning, give me one brief of what needs me — and don't add to the noise.
